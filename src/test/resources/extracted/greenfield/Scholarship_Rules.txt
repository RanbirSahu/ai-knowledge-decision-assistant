Greenfield University — Scholarship Rules
1. Overview
Greenfield University offers three financial awards: the Merit Scholarship, the Sports
Scholarship and the Need-Based Grant. All awards are applied to annual tuition and are
decided by the Scholarship Committee. Students may hold only one award at a time
unless the committee states otherwise.
2. Merit Scholarship
The Merit Scholarship rewards strong academic performance and covers 50 percent of
annual tuition. To be eligible, a student must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.5 and
must be in their second year of study or above. Students who already hold a sports
scholarship are not eligible for the Merit Scholarship, because the two awards cannot be
combined.
3. Sports Scholarship
The Sports Scholarship is awarded to students who represent the university at state level
or above. It covers 70 percent of annual tuition. Recipients must keep at least 75 percent
attendance at training sessions and maintain a minimum GPA of 2.5.
4. Need-Based Grant
The Need-Based Grant provides up to 40 percent tuition support for students from
low-income families. A student is eligible if the annual family income is below INR 600000
and the student has a minimum GPA of 2.5. Proof of income must be uploaded with the
application.
5. How to Apply
Step 1: Log in to the student portal and open the Scholarships tab. Step 2: Choose the
award you want and fill in the online form. Step 3: Upload your latest transcript and a
one-page statement of purpose. Step 4: Submit the form before the deadline of 15 July.
Results are announced within 3 weeks of the deadline by email.
6. Renewal
Awards are renewed every academic year. A Merit Scholarship holder must keep a GPA
of at least 3.3 to renew. Renewal forms are available in the student portal each May.
