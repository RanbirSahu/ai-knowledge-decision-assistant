Greenfield University — Admissions FAQ
1. Application Deadline
The application deadline for undergraduate admission is 30 June each year. Late
applications are accepted until 10 July with a late fee of INR 2000.
2. Documents Required
Applicants must submit their 12th grade marksheet, a government photo ID, two
passport-size photographs and the entrance exam scorecard. Documents must be
uploaded as PDF files in the admissions portal.
3. Application Fee
The application fee is INR 1500 for all programs and is not refundable. Payment can be
made online by card or net banking.
4. Entrance Exam
Admission is based on the Greenfield Entrance Test (GET) held every May. The test lasts
2 hours and has 100 multiple choice questions. There is no negative marking.
5. Contact
For admission queries write to admissions@greenfield.example or call the admissions
desk between 9 AM and 5 PM on weekdays.
