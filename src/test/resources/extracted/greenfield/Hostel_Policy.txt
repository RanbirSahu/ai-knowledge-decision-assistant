Greenfield University — Hostel Policy
1. Hostel Allotment
First-year students are guaranteed a hostel room if they apply before 30 June. Senior
students are allotted rooms by lottery in July.
2. Hostel Fees
Hostel fees are INR 85000 per year for a double room and INR 120000 per year for a
single room. Fees include electricity, Wi-Fi and laundry.
3. Curfew and Visitors
The hostel gate closes at 10 PM on weekdays and 11 PM on weekends. Visitors are
allowed in the common lounge until 6 PM and are not allowed in student rooms.
4. Mess
The mess serves breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian
meals are both available. Mess charges are included in the hostel fees.
