Zenith Electronics — Returns and Refund
 Policy
1. Overview
Zenith Electronics wants you to be happy with every purchase. This policy explains how
returns and refunds work for products bought from Zenith stores and the Zenith website.
2. Standard Returns (Unopened Items)
Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. The original
receipt is required.
3. Opened Items
Opened electronics may be returned within 14 days of purchase if the item is undamaged
and the original receipt is available. A 10 percent restocking fee applies to all opened
items.
4. Non-Returnable Items
Software licenses, gift cards and customised products cannot be returned.
5. Refund Timeline
Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7 business days after
the returned item is inspected.
6. How to Start a Return
Step 1: Visit My Orders on the Zenith website and select Return next to the item. Step 2:
Print the prepaid shipping label. Step 3: Pack the item with all accessories and drop it at
any partner courier point. Step 4: Track the return status in My Orders.
