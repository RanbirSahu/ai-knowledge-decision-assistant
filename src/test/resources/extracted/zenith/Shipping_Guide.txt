Zenith Electronics — Shipping Guide
1. Delivery Times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days
and costs INR 199 extra.
2. Shipping Charges
Shipping is free on orders above INR 999. Orders below INR 999 are charged INR 79 for
standard delivery.
3. Order Tracking
A tracking link is emailed once your order ships. You can also track the order in My Orders
on the Zenith website.
4. International Shipping
Zenith currently ships only within India. International delivery is not available.
