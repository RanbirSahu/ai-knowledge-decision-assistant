Zenith Electronics — Warranty Policy
1. Standard Warranty
Every Zenith product includes a standard warranty against manufacturing defects. Claims
must be filed within 12 months of purchase. Repairs are free of charge during this period.
2. Extended Protection Plan
The Extended Protection Plan adds 24 more months of cover for 8 percent of the product
price. It must be bought within 30 days of purchase and also covers accidental drops.
3. Exclusions
The warranty does not cover liquid damage, damage caused by unauthorised repairs or
normal wear and tear. Products with removed serial numbers are not covered.
4. How to Claim
Step 1: Register the claim at zenith.example/support with your order number. Step 2: Ship
the product using the free label. Step 3: Repairs take 5 to 10 business days.
